# Week 6: Evaluation and Comprehensive Business Reporting

## BizInsight AI – Final Evaluation, Business Insights and Recommendations

### Overview

Week 6 is the final stage of the BizInsight AI project. This week focuses on synthesizing the analytical work completed in the previous weeks and converting technical results into meaningful business insights and actionable recommendations for Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs).

The objective is to evaluate the predictive models and visual analytics developed during the project, summarize the major findings, and communicate the results in a clear and non-technical manner suitable for business stakeholders.

### Objectives

- Evaluate the performance of predictive models developed in previous weeks.
- Compare regression and time-series forecasting results using suitable evaluation metrics.
- Summarize major business insights obtained from data analysis and visualization.
- Convert quantitative findings into practical business recommendations.
- Present analytical outcomes in a simple and understandable format.
- Support business decision-making through data-driven insights.

### Evaluation Performed

The final evaluation includes:

- Linear Regression evaluation using RMSE and R² Score.
- Polynomial Regression evaluation using RMSE and R² Score.
- Prophet time-series forecasting evaluation using MAE and RMSE.
- Comparison of model performance.
- Analysis of major business insights.
- Identification of actionable areas for business improvement.

### Major Business Insights

The analysis identified several important findings, including:

- Stapler V3 was identified as a top revenue-generating product.
- Printer Paper V3 contributed the highest total product-level profit.
- Keyboard V3 was identified as a major loss-making product requiring profitability review.
- Customer analysis identified 131 churned customers, representing 26.2% of customers.
- The Office category showed the highest average profit margin.
- Water Bottle V2 had the lowest average closing stock and requires inventory monitoring.
- September 2024 was detected as an unusual revenue period through time-series anomaly analysis.

### Actionable Recommendations

Based on the analytical findings, BizInsight AI recommends:

1. Promote high-revenue products and maintain their availability.
2. Review pricing and cost structures for loss-making products.
3. Focus on customer retention strategies for churned customers.
4. Monitor low-stock products and plan timely replenishment.
5. Use revenue trends and forecasts for sales and resource planning.
6. Investigate unusual revenue movements and identify their business causes.

### Reporting Approach

The final report presents technical results in clear business language so that stakeholders can understand the findings without requiring advanced knowledge of data science or machine learning. The focus is on what happened, what the results mean for the business, and what actions can be taken next.

### Conclusion

Week 6 completes the BizInsight AI analytics workflow by connecting data analysis, visualization, predictive modeling, and time-series forecasting with practical business decision-making. The final evaluation demonstrates how business data can be transformed into useful insights that support growth, loss reduction, customer retention, inventory management, future planning, and better decision-making for SMEs.
