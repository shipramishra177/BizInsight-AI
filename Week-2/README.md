# Week 2 – Data Exploration and Cleaning

## Overview
Week 2 focused on exploring, cleaning, and validating the business datasets used for the BizInsight AI project.

## Work Completed
- Loaded and inspected Customer, Inventory, Product, and Sales datasets.
- Checked dataset structure, dimensions, data types, and basic statistics.
- Identified and handled missing values.
- Checked and removed duplicate records.
- Detected and inspected potential outliers using the IQR method.
- Corrected inconsistent text values and date formats.
- Converted date columns to appropriate datetime format.
- Validated business calculations such as Gross Sales, Discount Amount, Revenue, and Profit.
- Checked invalid and negative values.
- Performed final missing-value and duplicate checks.
- Prepared cleaned datasets for further analysis and dashboard development.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Google Colab

## Outcome
The datasets were cleaned, validated, and prepared for further exploratory analysis, visualization, predictive analytics, and AI-based business insights.

## Week 2 Status
Data exploration, cleaning, validation, and preparation of analysis-ready datasets were completed successfully.
