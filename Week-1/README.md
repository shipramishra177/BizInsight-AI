# Week 1 – Project Planning and Strategy

## Project Title
BizInsight AI – Business Analytics Platform for Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs)

## Overview
Week 1 focused on the planning and strategy of the BizInsight AI project. The project is designed to help Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) understand their business data and make better data-driven decisions.

## Project Objective
The main objective is to develop a business analytics platform that can analyze sales, customer, inventory, and financial data and provide meaningful insights, dashboards, predictive analysis, and actionable recommendations.

## Key Goals
- Analyze business data and identify important trends.
- Provide interactive dashboards and visual reports.
- Identify growth opportunities and reasons for losses.
- Support inventory and future business planning.
- Provide AI-based recommendations for decision-making.
- Act as a personal business analyst for SMEs.

## Expected Outcome
BizInsight AI will transform raw business data into understandable insights that can help SMEs improve business performance, reduce losses, plan future activities, and achieve sustainable growth.

## Week 1 Status
Project concept, objectives, scope, methodology, and overall development strategy were defined during Week 1.
