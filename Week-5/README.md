

## Overview
This week focuses on Advanced Analytics and Time-Series Analysis for the BizInsight-AI project.

## Objective
The objective is to analyze business data over time, identify temporal trends, detect seasonal patterns and anomalies, and use forecasting techniques to support future business decisions.

## Work Completed
- Loaded the cleaned sales dataset prepared during Week 2.
- Converted Order_Date into a proper datetime format.
- Sorted sales data chronologically.
- Aggregated sales data into a monthly time series.
- Created a Monthly Revenue Trend visualization.
- Applied a 3-Month Moving Average for trend analysis.
- Performed time-series decomposition using Statsmodels.
- Analyzed trend, seasonal, and residual components.
- Detected potential revenue anomalies using residual analysis.
- Visualized detected revenue anomalies.
- Prepared the dataset for Prophet forecasting.
- Installed and implemented Prophet.
- Built a time-series forecasting model.
- Generated a 6-month future revenue forecast.
- Evaluated forecasting performance using MAE and RMSE.
- Compared actual and forecasted revenue.
- Generated business insights from the time-series analysis.
- Developed practical recommendations for SMEs.

The analysis demonstrates how historical business data can be transformed into time-based insights and future revenue forecasts. The forecasting results can be used as decision-support information for business planning, while additional historical and business factors can be incorporated in future versions to improve forecasting performance.

## Conclusion
Week 5 demonstrates the application of advanced analytics and time-series techniques to the BizInsight-AI project. The completed analysis helps move the platform from descriptive business reporting toward trend analysis, anomaly detection, and predictive forecasting.
